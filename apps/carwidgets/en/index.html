How it works

A little more you, on your iPhone.

Choose a design

Explore car-inspired widget styles and pick a supported design in the app.

Make it personal

Customize options such as a vehicle photo, text and accent color. Available options depend on the selected widget style.

Add it to your Home Screen

Save your design in CarWidgets, add the widget to your iPhone Home Screen, and select the design you saved.

Access and purchases

Premium styles require Garage Pass, an in-app purchase. Review included features and the current local price in the app before purchasing.

Personalization options vary by widget style. Vehicle photos are supported in the Vehicle Style design.
