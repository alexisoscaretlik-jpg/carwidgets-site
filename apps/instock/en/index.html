How it works

Keep tabs on the products you choose.

Add a product link

Paste a public product page or share its link from Safari. Add a size, variant or target price if you want.

Follow stock and price

Keep the product pages you track together in one list. Checks run periodically on compatible pages.

Get matching alerts

With notifications enabled, receive a push alert when a check detects availability or a price matching your criteria.

Access and purchases

InStock Pro is optional. Review current access options, plans and local prices in the app before purchasing.

Support varies by retailer and page. Checks happen at intervals. An alert may arrive late, and an item may sell out before you open the retailer’s site.

InStock is independent of retailers and does not buy products on your behalf.
