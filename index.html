Frequently asked

How do I manage or cancel CarWidgets Pro?
Open Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → CarWidgets, where you can change or cancel anytime.

Trip sounds aren't playing on CarPlay.
iOS doesn't let apps detect CarPlay on their own. In the app, open Trip sounds → Arrival places for the one-time Shortcuts setup that plays your sound on connect/disconnect.

Why does the app ask for location?
Location powers live speed, range, weather, and the sound that plays when you arrive at a place you set. You can turn it off anytime in iOS Settings.
